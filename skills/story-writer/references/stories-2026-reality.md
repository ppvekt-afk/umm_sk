# Stories 2026 — verified

*Volatile. Re-verify quarterly (Instagram/Facebook ship Stories changes often).*

## The point: relationship engine, not reach
Instagram's own leadership (Mosseri) is explicit: **Stories are not your best tool for reaching new
people** — they're the **relationship-deepening engine** for *existing* followers. Sticker taps,
replies, and viewing history feed a **relationship signal** that bumps you to the front of someone's
tray *and* lifts your Feed/Reels ranking for them. So optimise for **intimacy + interaction**, not reach.

## Metrics that matter
**Completion rate, exits, replies, sticker taps, link taps, profile visits** — not impressions alone.
(Instagram unified surface counts under "Views" in 2026, but exits/taps remain the diagnostic.) Pair
with business outcomes (DMs, sign-ups, sales) for ROI.

## Interactive stickers — the single most effective tool
They turn passive viewers into **tappers** (a strong relationship signal) and double as audience research:
- **Poll / slider** — quick feedback, low-friction votes.
- **Quiz** (one correct answer) — educate, segment beginners vs advanced, product fit.
- **Question box** — invites replies; spin answers into an AMA follow-up sequence (lands in DMs).
- **Countdown** — a *real* deadline; tap-to-subscribe builds a warm retarget list.
- **Link sticker** (all accounts) — pair with an explicit **"Tap here"** overlay; **"add yours"** /
  **cutout** for participation. Reshare UGC tags as social proof.

## Structure + sequence
Per sequence: **hook (problem/outcome) → one proof point/step → an interactive sticker → one clear
next step.** **One goal per sequence** — don't ask three things. Build **3–5 frame arcs** / multi-day
mini-series; **one beat per frame**, each a distinct takeaway. Reply to Story replies **via DM** — the
real relationship (and the strongest distribution signal overall).

## Specs + cadence
**9:16, 1080×1920**; keep key content in the **safe zone (1080×1420)**, clear of UI; **≤15s per
segment**; images <4MB, video <30MB; **text sparingly**; **sound-off legible**, strong first frame.
**Daily** posting, ~**3–7 frames/day** (consistency > volume). Content mix ~**70% value / 20%
behind-the-scenes / 10% promo.**

## Highlights + cross-format
**Highlights** make proven sequences **evergreen** (add context) — the one durable lever on an
otherwise 24-hour surface. Stories are **spokes** off Reels/carousel hubs (repurpose a carousel slide
into a Story with a sticker the next day) — ties to `content-recycling` / `cross-platform-repurposing`.
