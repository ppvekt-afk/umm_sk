# The 2026 video toolbox — by job, not by hype

*Highly volatile. Re-verify quarterly — the text-to-video leaderboard reshuffles roughly weekly
and any rank claim has a half-life of months. Test on YOUR prompts, never vendor demo reels.*

**The router's first move is to name the JOB, then pick the tool. There is no single best AI
video tool — only a best tool per job.** Each tool below maps to a mini-skill (live or forthcoming).

## Job 1 — Generative B-roll / scenes (text- or image-to-video) → mini-skill: veo-3 (+ hubs)
- **Google Veo 3.1** — best all-rounder; strongest prompt adherence; the **only model with native
  synchronized dialogue** (48kHz, not just SFX); 4K, vertical + landscape; ~60s clips. Tiers
  Lite/Fast/Quality; ~$0.03–0.50/sec. *Pick for all-round quality and any talking/scripted scene.*
- **Runway Gen-4.5** — best **control surface** (motion brush, camera moves, reference character
  consistency); the ads/client-deliverable pick; ~16s max. *Led the benchmark at launch, since
  displaced — control, not raw rank, is why you'd choose it.*
- **Kling 3.0** — best **value + iteration**; native 4K/60fps; multi-shot storyboards; multilingual
  lip-sync; longer clips; ~$0.07/sec (much cheaper than Runway/old Sora). *Pick for 4K social at scale.*
- **Luma Ray3 / Ray3.14** — first 16-bit **HDR**; atmospheric/nature/mood; Ray3 Modify edits actor
  footage. *Pick for HDR finishing and calm, environment-heavy shots.* (Not "Dream Machine" anymore.)
- **MiniMax Hailuo 2.3** — fast (~2.5×) and cheap; good for ideation/volume. **Legal caution:** an
  active copyright lawsuit (Reuters, May 2026) — review rights/resemblance before commercial use.
- Context (don't over-route): Seedance 2.0 (top of benchmark but access-limited), Pika 2.5 (fast
  social effects, ~10s, lower realism), Gemini Omni (auto multi-shot), Wan/LTX/Hunyuan (open-weight,
  self-host). **Multi-model hubs** (Higgsfield, fal, Krea, Hedra, Leonardo) give one workflow across
  many models — useful for staying vendor-agnostic.

## Job 2 — Talking-head / avatar / faceless presenter → mini-skill: heygen (forthcoming)
- **HeyGen** (multilingual, personalized at scale, digital twins) and **Synthesia** (240+ avatars,
  explainers/training/localization). **D-ID** for real-time interactive avatars. *The #1 faceless
  format; a different job from generative B-roll.*

## Job 3 — Voiceover / narration → mini-skill: ai-voiceover (forthcoming)
- **ElevenLabs** — the leader for natural multilingual VO. Most generative video tools **need audio
  added in post** (only Veo 3.1 and Kling 2.6+ do native synced audio), so VO is usually a separate
  step.

## Job 4 — Edit / captions / long→Short clipping → mini-skill: captions-and-clipping (forthcoming)
- **Opus Clip, CapCut, Submagic** — turn long-form into Shorts and add sound-off captions (most
  social video is watched on mute). InVideo AI for fully automated assembly.

## Never route here
- **OpenAI Sora — DISCONTINUED** (app shut April 26 2026; API ends September 24 2026). Never list it
  as a buying route or build a new pipeline on it. It is the cautionary tale, not an option.

## Cross-cutting truths the router applies
Most models cap at short clips (5–16s; Veo ~60s; Kling longer) → **long pieces = stitch clips**, not
one take. **Concept-test** on cheap/fast tools (Pika, Hailuo, Luma), **finalize** on quality tools
(Veo, Kling, Runway). AI video is a **production accelerator, not a finished campaign** — always
plan post (trim, color, captions, audio, upscale).
