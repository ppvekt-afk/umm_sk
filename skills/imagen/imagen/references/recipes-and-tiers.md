# Tiers, recipes + two worked examples

## Tier selection (verify-quarterly)
| Tier | Use it for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Fast** (~$0.02) | drafts, variations, volume | cheapest/quickest; lock composition |
| **Standard** (~$0.04) | most finals at 2K | the balanced workhorse |
| **Ultra** (~$0.06) | hero shots, faces/skin, fine detail | best fidelity, zero waxy artifacts |

Pattern: **draft on Fast → finalize the keeper on Ultra.** Per-image, no subscription, license-clean.

## Photoreal social recipes (ground in brand-profile; review + disclose before publishing)
- **Product hero:** "[product], [material/finish], on [surface], [studio/ natural] lighting, [shot
  type + lens], shallow depth of field, [mood]. 4:5, 2K." (Ultra for reflective/detail.)
- **Lifestyle / UGC-style:** "[person/hands] using [product] in [real setting], natural window light,
  candid, 35mm, slight grain. 9:16." (No real identifiable person.)
- **Portrait / spokesperson (original):** "[described non-real person], [expression], [wardrobe],
  85mm portrait, soft key light, shallow DoF. 4:5." (Ultra for skin.)
- **Food:** "[dish], fresh, [garnish], overhead, soft daylight, marble surface, macro detail. 1:1."
- **Background / scene:** "[environment], [time of day], [lighting], no text, clean composition for
  overlay. 9:16." (Drop copy on later, or pair ideogram for type.)
- **Brand-consistent series:** lock subject/lighting/lens language; vary the scene; keep palette
  (brand-profile) — review for drift.

## Worked example 1 — product hero (blunt indie-founder voice)
```
JOB: app-on-desk hero for a Reel cover   TIER: Ultra   FORMAT: 4:5, 2K
PROMPT: "A matte-black phone on raw concrete showing a clean invoicing app, hard top light,
  sharp reflections, 50mm, shallow depth of field, dry minimal commercial style. No text on screen."
REVIEW: reflections + screen edges. SynthID + "AI-generated" disclosure on publish.
```

## Worked example 2 — lifestyle photo (warm studio voice)
```
JOB: warm desk lifestyle shot   TIER: Standard   FORMAT: 9:16, 2K
PROMPT: "Hands of a person at a tidy wooden desk with a coffee mug and an open laptop, soft morning
  window light, candid, 35mm, gentle bokeh, calm editorial mood." (no identifiable face)
REVIEW: hands + mug detail. DISCLOSURE: "AI-assisted image."
```

Both: read brand-profile first; photographic direction (no quality spam); right tier; native 2K;
reviewed for artifacts; SynthID + disclosure handled. Need exact type/poster → **ideogram**;
conversational edit → **nano-banana**; animate the still → **veo-3** (image-to-video). Publish →
upload to WoopSocial Media → attach via `scheduling-and-queue`.
